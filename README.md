# Poolside Estate

A family venture research and planning hub for **Poolside** — beginning with a premium poolside margarita concept and designed as a durable platform for future family ventures.

## Purpose
This repository is the source of truth for research, decisions, product development, regulatory planning, ownership architecture, economics, and launch execution.

## Current thesis
Build the brand and customer relationship first. Use appropriately licensed third parties for regulated alcohol production at launch, while evaluating vertical integration only when justified by economics and strategy.

## Important
Materials here are planning and research, not legal, tax, investment, or regulatory advice. Proposed entity/trust structures must be reviewed by qualified estate/tax and alcohol-regulatory professionals before implementation.

## Research tracks
- Brand & IP
- Poolside Margarita product development
- Tequila / RTD supply chain
- Federal & state alcohol compliance
- Family ownership & governance
- Unit economics & capital plan
- Distribution & go-to-market
- Non-alcoholic extensions
- Future family ventures

The website is designed to be the family-facing briefing layer; this repository remains the working source of truth.
