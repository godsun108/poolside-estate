# Poolside — Stage-Gate Roadmap

## G0 — Brand clearance
**Pass condition:** counsel-supported clearance strategy + domain/social plan.
No permanent packaging before pass.

## G1 — Product brief
**Pass condition:** family agrees on the flagship drinking experience.
Define: flavor, ABV, sweetness, carbonation, serving format, package, target MSRP.

## G2 — Supply feasibility
**Pass condition:** at least 3 qualified manufacturing pathways with written indicative economics, MOQs and lead times.

## G3 — Regulatory map
**Pass condition:** documented federal + first-state licensing/registration path and named responsible parties.

## G4 — Bench samples
**Pass condition:** blind family tasting identifies a formula worth commercializing.

## G5 — Economics
**Pass condition:** landed COGS, distributor economics, MSRP and working-capital model meet family thresholds.

## G6 — Legal architecture
**Pass condition:** estate/tax counsel + alcohol counsel approve ownership/control and contracts before regulated applications.

## G7 — Pilot
**Pass condition:** compliant formula/COLA/registrations as applicable; commercial pilot passes QA and shelf-life criteria.

## G8 — Controlled launch
**Pass condition:** launch geography, distributor/retail accounts, sampling calendar and measurement plan established.

## G9 — Scale decision
Scale only from depletion/reorder evidence, not shipment vanity metrics.
