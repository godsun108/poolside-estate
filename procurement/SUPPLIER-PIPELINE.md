## Qualification stages
**LEAD → CONTACTED → DOCUMENTED → SAMPLED → QUOTED → SHORTLIST → SELECTED**

A candidate cannot become DOCUMENTED without:
- legal facility identity;
- applicable permit/license confirmation;
- written capability confirmation;
- MOQ;
- development model;
- packaging capability;
- lead-time range.

A candidate cannot become SHORTLIST without:
- written quote;
- sample / benchmark evidence;
- QA discussion;
- regulatory pathway discussion;
- reference or demonstrated comparable production;
- economics entered in comparison model.
