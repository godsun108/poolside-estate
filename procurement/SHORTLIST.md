# Poolside Supplier Shortlist — v0.2

_Last updated: 2026-10-06. Public-source qualification only. No vendor has yet been contacted or endorsed._

## Tier 1 — U.S. RTD development / manufacturing

### 1. Funky Buddha — Oakland Park, Florida
**Role:** Spirit-based RTD development + manufacturing candidate  
**Public evidence:** Contract manufacturing page explicitly accepts "Spirit-Based RTD"; lists recipe development, formula scaling, sourcing, alcoholic beverage production, flash pasteurization, carbonation/nitrogen, high-speed canning, QA lab, TTB label/formula compliance, logistics. Can formats include 12oz standard and 12oz sleek.  
**Why Tier 1:** Broadest publicly documented match to Poolside requirements in Florida.  
**Unknowns:** DSP identity/permit, tequila handling/CRT approved-bottler status, MOQ, pricing, current lead time.  
**Source:** https://funkybuddha.com/contract/

### 2. Islamorada Beverages — Fort Pierce / Islamorada, Florida
**Role:** Florida RTD / distillery co-pack candidate  
**Public evidence:** Contract RTD page; contract distilling; variable-size/high-speed canning; published RTD MOQ 40 BBL / approximately 500 cases of 12oz cans. Fort Pierce facility listed at 3200 St. Lucie Blvd.  
**Why Tier 1:** Published startup-friendly MOQ and Florida distillery footprint.  
**Unknowns:** Tequila-based RTD experience, CRT approved-bottler status, package options, formula/TTB support, pricing.  
**Public contact:** info@islamoradabeverages.com · 305-508-9093  
**Source:** https://www.islamoradabeverages.com/contract-ready-to-drink/

### 3. Right Coast Brands — Framingham, Massachusetts
**Role:** Pilot / low-MOQ spirits-cocktail manufacturer  
**Public evidence:** Advertises commercial runs beginning at 100 cases / 2,400 cans and states it holds a DSP for spirits cocktails, with sleek/standard cans and QA/seam verification.  
**Why Tier 1:** Excellent candidate for prototype-to-commercial learning without a huge first inventory position.  
**Unknowns:** tequila sourcing/handling, CRT pathway, formula support depth, delivered economics to Florida.  
**Source:** https://rightcoast.co/

### 4. Craftsmith Beverage — Naperville, Illinois
**Role:** RTD cocktail contract manufacturer  
**Public evidence:** Explicitly lists RTD cocktails and spirit-based canned beverages; inquiry-to-proposal-to-production workflow.  
**Why Tier 1/2:** Clear category fit; needs deeper operational verification.  
**Unknowns:** DSP permit, tequila experience, MOQ, package formats, stabilization, pricing.  
**Source:** https://www.craftsmithbeverage.com/

### 5. BarrelRM — Torrance, California
**Role:** Spirits / RTD development and contract manufacturing  
**Public evidence:** RTD/RTS development, private-label spirits, contract manufacturing, compliance/distribution and R&D lab; explicitly lists agave/tequila among spirit capabilities.

## Recommended first wave
Contact **Funky Buddha, Islamorada, Right Coast, Casa Maestri, Elab Agave and Tequilera BSC** first.

This intentionally tests three models at once:
1. Florida spirit-RTD production;
2. low-MOQ U.S. pilot production;
3. Mexico tequila supply / private-label economics.

Do not contact all 12 simultaneously until the first six responses reveal which supply-chain model is strongest.
