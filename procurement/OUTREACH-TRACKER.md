## Outreach sequence
1. Send concise capability inquiry.
2. If tequila RTD confirmed, send full RFQ.
3. Request permit/facility details.
4. Schedule 20-minute technical call.
5. Request samples/references.
6. Enter written economics into quote model.
7. Score against stage-gate requirements.

## Technical call scorecard (1–5)
- tequila / spirit RTD experience
- formulation competence
- regulatory competence
- sensory/product quality
- MOQ fit
- packaging flexibility
- economics
- lead time
- QA maturity
- communication / transparency
