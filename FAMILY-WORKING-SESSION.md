# Family Working Session — 30 Minutes

## Objective
Leave the meeting with a clear product brief and explicit permission for the next diligence spend — not a commitment to launch.

## 1. The story — 5 min
Poolside is a family-estate venture concept beginning with a premium Margarita. The bigger goal is to build a durable family brand/IP asset and a repeatable venture-development system.

## 2. Taste vision — 10 min
Ask Poolside Dave:
- Describe your perfect Margarita in three words.
- Fresh/tart or soft/sweet?
- Still or lightly sparkling?
- Strong cocktail or longer/sessionable drink?
- Salt in the liquid, on the rim, or neither?
- Blanco character obvious or subtle?
- 8 oz, 12 oz sleek, or pour-over-ice format?
- What existing Margarita is closest?
- What would make you reject one immediately?
- What price feels cheap / fair / expensive for a 4-pack?

Record answers verbatim.

## 3. Brand world — 5 min
Choose which words fit:
- resort
- backyard
- yacht club
- Florida
- California
- Mexico
- tropical
- timeless
- playful
- premium
- relaxed
- family
- irreverent
- elegant

Then choose 3 words Poolside must **never** feel like.

## 4. Family architecture — 5 min
Do not choose legal entities in this meeting. Agree only on principles:
- family control / succession matters;
- valuable IP should be protected from unnecessary operating risk;
- alcohol compliance comes before clever structuring;
- estate/tax and alcohol counsel review the final structure.

## 5. Permission to proceed — 5 min
Authorize research/sample budget only:
- trademark clearance quote;
- prototype/formulation conversations;
- manufacturer RFQs;
- packaging samples;
- regulatory counsel consultation.

No production commitment yet.
