# Regulatory & Supply-Chain Diligence — v0.1

_Last updated: 2026-10-06. Research only; counsel review required before implementation._

## Executive conclusion
**CONFIRMED:** A Poolside tequila-based RTD can be built without Poolside owning a distillery. The practical launch routes to price are:

1. **U.S. formulation/canning:** source authentic certified tequila, import it (directly or through a licensed partner), then formulate/package the cocktail at a properly licensed U.S. distilled spirits plant/co-packer.
2. **Mexico finished-goods route:** work with an authorized Mexican producer/packer to produce a compliant finished tequila cocktail and import the finished product.

Final permit responsibility depends on who takes title, imports, manufactures, and sells at wholesale.

## Federal — confirmed
### Tequila authenticity
TTB recognizes Tequila as a distinctive product of Mexico. Tequila exported to the U.S. requires a Certificate of Authenticity for Exports issued through the CRT.
Primary source: https://www.ttb.gov/import-export/itd/certificate-of-age-and-origin-requirements-for-imported-alcohol-beverages

### Cocktail formula
TTB's current formula tool states that Distilled Spirits Specialty Products/Cocktails, including RTD mixed drinks, require formula approval in applicable imported specialty/cocktail cases. Formula approval can precede COLA/label approval.
Sources:
- https://www.ttb.gov/formulation
- https://www.ttb.gov/formulation/do-i-need-a-formula-spirits-imported
- https://www.ttb.gov/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/distilled-spirits/labeling-and-formulation-approval

### Importing / wholesaling
Businesses importing beverage alcohol commercially, or purchasing/selling alcohol at wholesale, generally require the applicable TTB Basic Permit before commencing that business.
Source: https://www.ttb.gov/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/wholesaler/applying-for-a-permit

### Federal excise tax
TTB lists distilled-spirits federal excise tax by proof gallon. Current reduced rate for eligible first 100,000 proof gallons is $2.70/proof gallon; general rate is $13.50/proof gallon. Eligibility/allocation must be modeled with the actual producer/importer arrangement.
Source: https://www.ttb.gov/taxes/tax-audit/taxes-and-filing/tax-rates

## Mexico / CRT — confirmed
CRT's certification materials explicitly contemplate **non-producer trademark holders**. A non-producer brand holder works with an Authorized Producer and a Joint Responsibility Agreement; brand certification and labeling requirements apply.
Source: https://www.crt.org.mx/en/certification-body/

CRT also maintains a searchable/downloadable registry of certified tequila companies and brands, useful for building the producer candidate list.
Source: https://www.crt.org.mx/empresas-y-marcas-certificadas/

## Florida launch — confirmed
If Florida is an initial market:
- applicable alcohol brands/labels must be registered before sale/movement in the state;
- distilled-spirit brand registration is listed at $30 per brand/label;
- Florida states alcoholic beverages must be distributed by Florida-licensed distributors;
- spirit registrants must be the manufacturer or legally authorized exclusive agent.
Sources:
- https://www.myfloridalicense.com/CheckListDetail.asp?XACT_DEFN_ID=7172&clientCode=4011&xactCode=1030
- https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/abt/rules_statutes/license_types.pdf
