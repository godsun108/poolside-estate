# Unit Economics Model — Inputs v0.1

This is the model specification. No fabricated economics are presented as facts.

## Core equation
Net revenue to Poolside
- liquid/spirit
- citrus/flavor/sweetener
- can + lid + decoration
- secondary packaging
- co-packing/conversion
- federal excise tax (as applicable)
- state excise/taxes (as applicable)
- freight / 3PL
- distributor/broker deductions
- samples, breakage, promos and depletion support
= **contribution margin**

## Known regulatory input
TTB currently publishes distilled-spirit FET on a proof-gallon basis, including an eligible reduced rate of $2.70/proof gallon for the first 100,000 proof gallons and a $13.50 general rate. Actual eligibility depends on the production/import structure.
Source: https://www.ttb.gov/taxes/tax-audit/taxes-and-filing/tax-rates

## Competitive anchors — research, not target price
Current market examples span materially different propositions:
- Jose Cuervo announced 2026 canned cocktails at 8-can SRP $18.99, 4-pack SRP $9.99, singles $2.99.
- Cutwater Lime Margarita is commonly positioned around 12.5% ABV and a premium 4-pack.
These are category anchors only; Poolside pricing must be derived from product and margin architecture.
