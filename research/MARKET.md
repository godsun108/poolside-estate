# Market Evidence — Why Margarita / Why RTD?

_Last updated: 2026-10-06._

## Current evidence

### U.S. spirits RTDs are growing
NIQ's U.S. 2025 year-in-review data (52 weeks ending Jan. 3, 2026) reported:
- tequila-based spirits RTDs: **$659.0M**, +30.0% vs prior year;
- Margarita: **12%** of spirits-RTD dollars by cocktail type, +45.3% vs prior year;
- vodka remained larger, but tequila was one of the fastest-growing major spirit bases.

Source: https://www.winebusiness.com/content/file/NIQ_US_BevAl_-_2025_Year_in_Review%281%29.pdf

### Margarita is already culturally understood
NIQ reported in 2026 U.S. on-premise research that Margarita was the most commonly ordered cocktail among cocktail drinkers in its study, at 54%.

Source: https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/five-consumer-trends-shaping-us-on-premise-spirit-sales/

### Occasion-led positioning matters
NIQ's 2026 RTD analysis argues that strong RTD concepts are increasingly occasion-led, with consumers selecting different beverages by venue/occasion. This directly supports testing **Poolside as an occasion platform**, rather than treating it only as a flavor name.

Source: https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/is-rtd-a-growth-opportunity-for-your-brand/

### Category caution
IWSR describes RTD as a maturing, increasingly competitive category. Growth does **not** mean any new entrant wins. Brand strength, channel fit, repeat purchase and differentiated product quality matter.

Sources:
- https://www.theiwsr.com/insight/six-key-drivers-shaping-beverage-alcohol-in-2026-and-beyond/
- https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/commentary/2026/why-brand-strength-is-the-real-battleground-in-ready-to-drink/

## Strategic interpretation
**PROPOSAL:** Poolside should not compete by being “another canned margarita.” The potential wedge is:
1. an instantly legible **occasion** (poolside);
2. a familiar, high-demand **cocktail** (Margarita);
3. a real-spirit / quality cue;
4. an aesthetic and hospitality system strong enough to extend beyond one SKU;
5. eventual zero-proof participation in the same occasion.

## Product hypothesis to test
A bright, genuinely lime-forward tequila Margarita with restrained sweetness and a clean finish, optimized for long outdoor occasions rather than maximum alcohol delivery.

This is a hypothesis, not yet a formula.

## Competitive benchmarks to taste blind
Build a benchmark panel spanning:
- high-ABV still “bar-style” Margarita;
- lower-ABV sparkling Margarita;
- mainstream value leader;
- premium spirit-based challenger;
- fresh house Margarita control.

Score:
- tequila authenticity
- lime quality
- sweetness
- acidity
- aroma
- finish
- sessionability
- “would order another?”
- “does this belong poolside?”
- willingness to pay

## Key strategic unknown
**ABV is not decided.** Test at least a lower/sessionable and a fuller-strength prototype. The brand should optimize the occasion and repeat enjoyment, not chase ABV as a marketing claim.
