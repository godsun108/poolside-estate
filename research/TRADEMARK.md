# Brand & Trademark Diligence — POOLSIDE

_Last updated: 2026-10-06. Preliminary research only — not a legal clearance opinion._

## Gate 0 conclusion
**STATUS: NOT CLEARED.**

Do not order permanent packaging, sign a long-term producer agreement under the name, or represent to the family that POOLSIDE is legally available until a comprehensive trademark clearance is completed.

## Why
USPTO states that likelihood of confusion can arise where marks are similar in sound, appearance, meaning or commercial impression and the goods/services are related. An exact-match search alone is insufficient.
Primary source: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion

### Historical result
A federal standard-character POOLSIDE registration (Reg. 3050626 / Serial 78554289) covered wine in International Class 33. Third-party status sources indicate it was cancelled under Section 8 in 2012.

Cancellation of a federal registration is **not a clearance opinion** and does not establish that no common-law or later rights exist.

### Related beverage result
A later POOLSIDE application (Serial 90641250) has existed for beer in Class 32. Current status and prosecution history must be confirmed directly in USPTO records during formal clearance.

## Required clearance scope
- USPTO federal records: POOLSIDE and phonetic/visual variants.
- State trademark/business-name records in intended launch states.
- Common-law web/social/domain searches.
- Alcohol databases, retailer listings and distributor portfolios.
- Related Class 32 beverages and Class 33 alcoholic beverages.
- Hospitality / bar / resort uses if future brand expansion makes them relevant.
- Domain and primary social handles.

## Filing strategy
If counsel concludes risk is acceptable and the family has bona fide intent to use the mark, USPTO permits a Section 1(b) intent-to-use application before actual use in commerce.
Source: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply/intent-use-itu-applications

## Working rule
Until clearance: **Poolside = project codename / working brand.**
