THE 5-MINUTE FAMILY BRIEF

What are we building?

01 · Flagship

Poolside Margarita

A premium ready-to-drink margarita concept centered on real tequila, bright lime, restrained sweetness and effortless poolside service.

02 · Brand World

More than alcohol

The underlying territory is the poolside occasion. Future possibilities include tequila, Paloma, Ranch Water, zero-proof drinks, hospitality, merchandise and experiences.

03 · Institution

Built for generations

Poolside is also a test case for a repeatable family venture system: research, IP, governance, operating companies, capital allocation and succession.

WORKING THESIS

Own what compounds. Contract what doesn't need to be owned yet.

At launch, the strategic objective is to own the valuable brand assets — customer relationship, trademarks, recipes/specifications, creative system and commercial knowledge — while qualified licensed partners handle regulated production. Vertical integration remains an option, not a prerequisite.

OPEN QUESTIONS

What we need to learn next.

Product

Dave's ideal margarita profile, ABV, sweetness, carbonation, serving format, package size and target retail price.

Supply

Qualified tequila partners, RTD co-packers, minimum runs, landed cost, lead times and quality controls.

Compliance

TTB formula/label pathway, federal permits, state launch strategy, distribution model and ownership disclosures.

Economics

COGS, excise taxes, freight, distributor/retailer margins, marketing allowance, gross margin and working capital.

DECISION PRINCIPLES

The guardrails.

Quality before SKU count. The first product must deserve the brand.

Longevity before shortcuts. Structure decisions for decades, not just launch day.

Control the durable assets. Brand, IP, knowledge and customer relationship compound.

Responsible occasion. Poolside celebrates connection, not intoxication; zero-proof belongs in the long-term world.

Evidence before commitment. Research, sample, model and validate before meaningful capital is deployed.

STATUS · DISCOVERY

Poolside is at the beginning.

Nothing here requires the family to commit capital yet. The immediate mission is diligence: clear the brand, define the drink, identify partners, understand regulation and prove the economics.
